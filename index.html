About

I study how tissues respond to infection. As a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Agaisse Lab at the University of Virginia School of Medicine, I map Shigella invasion and the local immune response in the colon, combining multiplex RNAscope on FFPE tissue, quantitative image analysis, and RNA-seq of infected tissue and human biopsies.

Before UVA, I worked on microbial genomics, metagenomics, and antimicrobial resistance across environmental and One Health settings in India and Finland, and contributed to the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report and the UN Interagency Coordination Group on Antimicrobial Resistance.

13+ years of research · 15 peer-reviewed publications · 5 book chapters · 15+ students and staff trained

Selected research

Spatial mapping of Shigella invasion

Spatial and temporal mapping of colonic invasion in the infant rabbit model: which cells are invaded, in what order, and how macrophage and neutrophil responses are organized around invasion sites.

Spatial biologyImmunology Preprint (bioRxiv 2026)

Serology QC and power analysis in R

A reproducible analysis of a public serosurvey (3,080 sera): data QC, assay cutoff evaluation with a mixture model, seroprevalence with confidence intervals, and power and sample-size calculations for study design.

BiostatisticsR Code on GitHub

Antimicrobial resistance across One Health niches

A review of how antimicrobial resistance moves between animal, agricultural, and environmental reservoirs, informing international AMR policy discussions.

AMROne Health Environmental Microbiology 2022

Genomics of Indian rabies virus isolates

Oxford Nanopore whole-genome sequencing of canine rabies virus isolates and phylogenetic analysis for One Health surveillance.

GenomicsNanopore

Microbial communities in wastewater treatment

Metagenomic analysis of how activated-sludge communities adapt and which degradation pathways they carry, to design more efficient industrial treatment.

Metagenomics Functional & Integrative Genomics 2019
