Microbiologist + Biotechnologist

About

I bring spatial omics and AI/ML-driven analysis to the study of microbes — decoding how microbial life behaves at single-cell resolution, whether inside an infected host or across an entire wastewater ecosystem.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Agaisse Lab at the University of Virginia School of Medicine, where I investigate Shigella infection biology using an infant rabbit model, with a focus on tissue niches, host responses, and infection-associated remodeling.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Biotechnology at RTMNU, Nagpur, with research conducted at CSIR-NEERI on discovering novel oxygenases in effluent-treatment-plant metagenomes. My broader experience includes wastewater bioremediation, environmental antimicrobial resistance, microbial ecology, biodiversity, and One Health research, including work at the Finnish Environment Institute and the Ashoka Trust for Research in Ecology and the Environment.

I have contributed to the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report and the UN Interagency Coordination Group on Antimicrobial Resistance, mentored more than a dozen master's students, and presented research at international conferences. My long-term goal is to develop data-driven approaches that connect microbial function, host biology, and environmental transmission.

Selected Research

A selection of research spanning host-pathogen biology, genomics, environmental metagenomics, and antimicrobial resistance.

Shigellosis in the Infant Rabbit Model

Project Link

Spatial and temporal mapping of colonic mucosa invasion in the infant rabbit model of shigellosis, combining RNA-seq and spatial transcriptomics to characterize how Shigella flexneri invades and spreads through host tissue during infection. Presented at the EMBO Workshop: Shigella – From Biology to Prevention, Institut Pasteur, Paris, April 2026. Agaisse Lab, UVA School of Medicine · Manuscript in prep.

Whole Genome Analysis

Genomics of Indian Rabies Virus Isolates

Project Link

Genome sequencing of five Indian canine rabies virus isolates using Oxford Nanopore Technologies (Kumar et al., 2022), and phylogenetic analysis of canine RABV whole-genome sequences from Asian and Middle Eastern countries (Jadeja & Vanak, 2023, book chapter), contributing to viral surveillance and One Health research in India.

Genome Sequencing of Biosurfactant-Producing Strains

Project Link

Genome sequencing and analysis of Bacillus sp. AKBS9 and Acinetobacter sp. AKBS16, mapping the genetic organization of aromatic compound-cleaving oxygenases relevant to biosurfactant production and bioremediation potential. Jadeja, Moharir & Kapley, 2019, Applied Biochemistry and Biotechnology.

Metagenomics

Decoding Microbial Intelligence in Wastewater

Project Link

A metagenomic study decoding how microbial communities in activated sludge adapt and function, mapping metabolic pathways onto the KEGG global metabolism atlas, with implications for designing more efficient industrial wastewater treatment systems. NEERI, Nagpur · 2019, Functional & Integrative Genomics.

Gut to Mud: AMR Across Niches

Project Link

A review of how antimicrobial resistance disseminates between animal, agricultural, and environmental niches across the One Health interface, informing international AMR policy discussions including the UN IACG. Jadeja & Worrich, 2022, Environmental Microbiology.

Knowledge-Based Bioremediation Strategies

Project Link

A book chapter reviewing sequencing platforms and metagenomic approaches used to design targeted, knowledge-based bioremediation strategies for contaminated environments. Jadeja, Yadav & Kapley, 2021, Recent Advances in Environmental Management.
